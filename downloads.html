🤖 Android .apk

Downloads as an .apk file. Android will warn about installing from an unknown source the first time - that's expected for an app outside the Play Store, not a sign anything is wrong. You'll need to tap "Install anyway" (wording varies by phone).

🖥️ Windows .msix

Downloads as an .msix installer. Because it isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate, Windows SmartScreen may show a warning the first time you run it - choose "More info" then "Run anyway" to continue.
