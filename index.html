What is this?

CharacterTracker is a companion app for Lorien Trust, a UK fantasy LARP. It handles the parts of character creation and progression that are easy to get wrong or slow to work out by hand: Character Skill point spending, browsing and purchasing Occupational Skills by Guild list, tracking Loresheet progression (Awakened, Essence Creatures, and Ritual Creatures), and laying it all out on a printable character card that matches the real thing.

It can track multiple characters, works offline, and (optionally) syncs your characters to your account so you can pick up where you left off on another device.

Built by Edd Robinson for the Lorien Trust community. Not an official Lorien Trust product.
