Guides

A few pointers to get started. More guides will be added here over time.

Getting started: your first character

  1. From the Home screen, tap New Character and give them a name and starting Race.
  2. Open Character Skills to spend your 16 Character Skill points across Weapon, Armour, Knowledge, and Power skills.
  3. Open Occupational Skills to browse and purchase skills from your Guild's list using your OSP (Occupational Skill Points) - use the search box, the "only eligible" toggle, and the sort button (Tier / Guild / Name) to find what you're after.
  4. If your character is Awakened, or becomes an Essence or Ritual Creature, open Loresheet Skills to track that progression separately from your Occupational Skills.
  5. Open the Character Card at any time to see how your character prints out, front and back - this updates live as you make changes.

Signing in and syncing between devices

Creating an account from the Account page lets your characters follow you between devices. You'll get a confirmation email to verify your address before you can sign in for the first time.
